# Small-Business Product Quality Standard

KH_Aw does not accept a screen merely because required functions exist. Each project must provide physical evidence for every applicable quality question.

1. Does the first viewport communicate the product value and next action within five seconds?
2. Do the screens and functions express a meaningful difference from competitors?
3. Do images increase understanding, trust, persuasion, or information value?
4. Does motion improve hierarchy, feedback, navigation, or brand experience?
5. Is the first mobile viewport clear, legible, and persuasive?
6. Are loading, empty, error, success, permission, offline, and restricted states designed?
7. Are the discovery, registration, editing, deletion, inquiry, and completion flows natural for this project?
8. Are interface elements connected to real data and logic?
9. Are accessibility and reduced-motion behavior implemented?
10. Is performance acceptable on low-spec devices and slower networks?
11. Are license, privacy, and security risks controlled?
12. Is there a free or low-cost operating path where the user has not approved paid dependencies?
13. Can analytics, logs, and error evidence support later improvement?
14. Are testing and release steps repeatable and reproducible?
15. Is the complete project-specific journey implemented rather than a single sample page?

Scoring must be project-specific and evidence-backed. Missing evidence cannot receive a high score, and automated structure checks cannot replace independent semantic design review.
